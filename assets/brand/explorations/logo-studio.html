indah — pick a mark

indah is the soft, flowing counterpoint to the suite's angular ketupat — sharing its minimalism and the warm palette, but curvy and floral, because indah means beautiful. The mark carries colour; the indah wordmark stays one ink colour. Each shows its lockup, an on-ink chip, and monochrome + favicon sizes. Toggle light/dark, top-right.

Option A

Bunga

A four-petal bloom — two magenta petals, two teal, around a soft centre. The most literal reading of indah = beautiful, and it ties the mark straight to the theme's bougainvillea magenta.
